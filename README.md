Ek samay ki baat hai, ek ghane jungle mein chuha, tota aur khargosh saath rehte the. Chuha apni chalaki ke liye mashhoor tha, tota apni tez yaadasht aur bolne ki kala ke liye, aur khargosh apni tez daud ke liye. Jungle mein jab bhi koi mushkil aati, teeno milkar uska hal nikaalte. Ek baar jungle mein aag lagne ka khatra hua, toh chuhe ne rasta dhoonda, tote ne sabko chetavani di, aur khargosh ne sab jaanwaron ko surakshit jagah tak pahunchaya. Is tarah, teeno ki dosti aur samajhdari se jungle hamesha surakshit raha.
